# Blazorly 0.17.5

Delegation is on a shorter leash. A runaway session could fan out into dozens of nested sub-agents, reviewers reviewing reviewers, and progress reports steering the lead in circles — this release caps all of it, fixes a bug that could spawn a sub-agent with no model route at all, and removes the never-used System One decision layer.

- **One level of delegation.** Sub-agents can no longer delegate, spawn teammates, or call review tools — those tools don't even appear in a child's tool list. Children do the work; coordinating stays with the lead. Swarm, workflow, and team workers are unaffected: they were always one level deep.
- **Stopping a chat stops its workers.** Cancelling a session now cancels its whole live sub-agent tree, deepest first, instead of leaving delegated work running invisibly in the background.
- **A hard budget per sub-agent.** Each delegated child gets 100 tool calls per run. Past that it must stop and hand its summary back; the lead can resume it deliberately if the work needs more. A child can always report back, even over budget.
- **Review is fresh, read-mostly, and once per session.** The reviewer no longer forks your whole conversation — your scope text is its entire brief — and it gets a read-and-check toolset (`read`, `grep`, `glob`, `bash`). A session gets one completed review; after that the lead acts on the verdict instead of re-reviewing.
- **Only final teammate reports interrupt you.** Progress reports are recorded in the team mailbox but never splice into the lead's conversation. A teammate's report reaches the chat when it's marked final — finished or blocked.
- **Fixed: sub-agents born without a model route.** Models often emit `"provider": ""` for optional `subagent_start` fields, and the empty string silently overrode the inherited route, so the child failed with `NO_ADAPTER` on every message while its parent kept re-sending work to it. Empty overrides now mean "no override", and when a child does fail, the lead sees the actual error instead of "(no final message)". Foreground spawns also honour provider/model overrides now — they were silently ignored before.
- **Sub-agent session IDs can't collide.** Child IDs are random instead of a sequential counter, so a restart can no longer fail spawns against IDs already in the durable store.
- **Removed: System One decision model.** The optional TypeSafe/JEV-1 classifier (`enableSystemOne`, risk-gate, tool-gate, `blazorly decisions`, `GET /api/decisions`) was never enabled in practice and is gone, settings UI included. Auto-plan keeps the deterministic complexity heuristic that actually made the decisions all along.

## Upgrade notes

- Restart the app to pick up the new behaviour.
- No settings migration is required. Leftover `enableSystemOne`, `systemOne*`, `enableRiskGate`, `enableToolGate`, and `autoPlanEngageAt/SkipAt` keys in `settings.json` are ignored, as is a `system-one` entry in `disabledPlugins`.
- The `SYSTEMONE_API_KEY` environment variable is no longer read.
- Expect delegation to feel stricter: a sub-agent asking to spawn its own helper, a second `review` in one session, or a child past its tool budget now gets a clear refusal the lead can act on, rather than a silent fan-out.
